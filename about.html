About

Robotics • Software • Systems

I enjoy building things that actually work — whether that’s a robot navigating a space, a system validating sensor data, or a simple web app that solves a real problem.

My background sits somewhere between robotics, software engineering, and hands-on system debugging. I like understanding how things behave in the real world, not just how they’re supposed to work on paper.

Recently, I’ve been building projects around mobile robotics, computer vision, and lightweight web apps — focusing on practical applications and clean, usable designs.

Outside of projects, I enjoy running, training for endurance events, and building small tools or apps that make everyday things a little easier.
